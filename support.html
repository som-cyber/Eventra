Good to know

Help Center

How do I register for an event?

Open an event and choose Register. Complete the form to save your registration in this browser.

Where can I find my registrations?

Choose My Registrations in the navigation. You can view or cancel a saved registration there.

What is the QR event pass for?

It is a handy reference for your saved registration. This demo pass is not verified for entry, and scanning it does not validate a ticket.

Can I change my registration?

This sample site does not support editing a registration. You can cancel it from My Registrations and submit the form again.

Does checkout take real payment?

No. Checkout only simulates success or decline for the prototype. Do not enter payment credentials; no money is moved.

Information saved in this demo

ÉVENTRA stores event registrations, demo payment method/status/reference, favorites, theme settings, discovery interests, and quiz preferences in your browser's Local Storage. It does not collect card, bank, or UPI credentials. This version has no account system or application backend.

Your choices

You can manage registrations on the My Registrations page. Clearing this site's browser data removes information saved on this device. Local Storage is not shared across browsers or devices.

External resources

Some images, fonts, and the QR-code library may load from third-party services. Their providers may receive routine connection information when your browser requests those resources.
